# Pixel PCB v0.2: plan and handoff

Working notes for the v0.2 layout, written 2026-11-17 so the work can continue on
a machine that has KiCad 10. Once v0.2 is ordered, move what's settled into the
README and delete this file.

## Where things stand

- **v0.1 bring-up** (PR #16, branch `pixel-v0.1-bringup`; this branch is based on
  it): 2 of 5 boards work. Both custom footprints are proven, the data order is
  W,R,G,B, and chaining works. The boards draw about 15.4 mA per channel at 12 V,
  VDD is 5.235 V, and the white headroom knee is at about 10 V. Details are in
  `production/v0.1/MANIFEST.md` → Results.
- **Still to test on v0.1** (planned for 2026-11-18):
  - Brightness inside a real house. If it's too dim, the LED choice or count
    could change, which would affect the layout.
  - The 12 V-on-DATA_IN and reversed-plug protection tests. These decide R2
    (value only; the 0402 footprint stays).
- **Holder:** coupon v0.3 is printed but not yet tested. The user expects it to
  pass, so the layout is starting now. If the jaws or cleats change, expect some
  rework of the keep-outs or notch.
- **JLCPCB answered (2026-11-17):** v0.2 needs **Standard PCBA** on a 4 x 4
  panel. The headers are hand- or selective-soldered at $0.0164 per joint plus
  $3.58 per order. There is no fixed clearance between pins and SMT parts; it is
  checked in DFM. Details are in the README, under Pixel PCB.

## v0.2 changes

All of these come from the README. Sources: "Feedback for Pixel PCB v0.2" (House
holder section), the Pixel PCB section, and Interconnect.

1. **Headers:** vertical, on the bottom, in two stacked rows through the center.
   - J1: male XFCN PZ254V-11-03P, **C2937625**. Pin row east-west, centered on
     x = 0, at holder y = +2.1 mm.
   - J2: female 8.5 mm XFCN PM254V-11-03-H85, **C541850**. Pin row at holder
     y = −1.525 mm.
   - KiCad Y is flipped relative to the holder model. Confirm which row sits
     toward the cord notch (south) against `hardware/holder/params.scad` and
     `pixel_mock.scad` before placing.
   - Footprints: KiCad stock `PinHeader_1x03_P2.54mm_Vertical` and
     `PinSocket_1x03_P2.54mm_Vertical`, placed on B.Cu.
   - Keep power on the middle pin. Either pin order works.
2. **Top-side keep-outs:**
   - The six pin tails stick up about 1.4 - 1.6 mm through the middle, in a
     roughly 5.7 x 4.3 mm patch.
   - Keep about **1.5 - 2 mm** of clearance from those pins to SMT parts until
     JLCPCB gives a number.
   - Keep the edge ring clear for the holder's hook lips: top-side parts at
     r ≤ 9.25 mm.
3. **Orientation feature** (notch, flat or small hole) at the south edge, to match
   the holder. Check `hardware/holder/holder.scad` for what the holder expects.
4. **Center the LED cluster.** v0.1's sat about 2.5 mm south of center.
5. **White solder mask.** v0.1 was green.
6. **Silkscreen version: "v0.2".** The release goes in `production/v0.2/` with a
   MANIFEST, following v0.1's pattern.
7. **Keep the v0.1 circuit unless tomorrow's tests change it.** That covers U1
   (WS2814F), D1 (BAT54S), R1 2.7k, R2 1k (TBD), R3 100, R4 120, C1 100n, C2 1u
   50 V, and D2-D4 HL-5050RGBW. Update J1/J2 LCSC numbers in the schematic.

## Assembly (decided: Standard PCBA, 4 x 4 panel)

- Build a panel of 4 x 4 boards with mouse bites, 5 mm rails and fiducials. It
  must be at least 70 x 70 mm and at most 250 x 250 mm. Two panels of 16 cover 22
  houses plus spares.
- Check placement feasibility first, because the pin keep-out may conflict with
  the edge keep-out:
  - The pin pads span about x ±3.4 mm and y −2.4 to +3.0 mm (1.7 mm pads).
  - A 5 x 5 mm LED placed east or west of them, even 1 mm away, reaches past
    r 9.25 mm at its corners.
  - North and south placements are tight too (about r 9.3 with 1 mm clearance).
  - The holder allows D3 to reach r 9.62 mm in the south mouth.
  - If the LEDs don't fit, raise it with the user before shrinking clearances.
- Low parts (0402 resistors and capacitors, SOT-23, FSOP-8) can sit nearer the
  pins than the 1.6 mm-tall LEDs, because JLCPCB's concern is iron access.
- The user is not set up for hand soldering. JLCPCB should assemble everything,
  including the through-hole headers.

## Tooling notes (from v0.1)

- The project targets **KiCad 10**. The v0.1 files are saved in KiCad 10's format.
- **Routing:** export DSN with `pcbnew.ExportSpecctraDSN`, route with
  Freerouting's bundled launcher, then import the session in KiCad (File →
  Import → Specctra Session). Konnect's own DSN export refused the round board.
- **Rules:** `pixel.kicad_dru` and the board setup enforce JLCPCB's limits. Power
  netclass 0.4 mm. Signals 0.2 mm, with VDD allowed down to 0.15 mm near U1.
- **JLCPCB export:** Konnect's `export_manufacturing_package` with `fab_house`
  jlcpcb, BOM fields `Value,Reference,Footprint,LCSC`, and
  `jlcpcb_cpl_corrections_path` = `jlcpcb_cpl_corrections.json` (U1 +270,
  D1 +180). Check JLCPCB's placement preview.
- **LCSC lookups:** the kicad-happy `lcsc` skill's `search_lcsc.py` and
  `fetch_datasheet_lcsc.py`. The jlcsearch `/headers/list.json?pitch=2.54&num_pins=3`
  endpoint lists header candidates. `https://wmsc.lcsc.com/ftps/wm/product/detail?productCode=Cxxxx`
  gives live LCSC stock.
