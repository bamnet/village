# Pixel PCB v0.2: plan and handoff

Working notes for the v0.2 layout, written 2026-11-17 so the work can continue on
a machine that has KiCad 10. Once v0.2 is ordered, move what's settled into the
README and delete this file.

## Where things stand

- **v0.1 bring-up** (PR #16, branch `pixel-v0.1-bringup`; this branch is based on
  it): 2 of 5 boards work. Both custom footprints are proven, the data order is
  W,R,G,B, and chaining works. The boards draw about 15.4 mA per channel at 12 V,
  VDD is 5.235 V, and the white headroom knee is at about 10 V. Details are in
  `production/v0.1/MANIFEST.md` → Results.
- **v0.1 tests done (2026-11-18):** brightness inside a house passes (keep 3 LEDs;
  v170, a stand-in for 2 LEDs, looked dimmer). The offset plug fails with 1k, so
  **R2 → 4.7k (C25900)**, a value change only. The reversed plug passes. Details
  are in the MANIFEST and the README.
- **Holder:** coupon v0.3 is printed but not yet tested. The user expects it to
  pass, so the layout is starting now. If the jaws or cleats change, expect some
  rework of the keep-outs or notch.
- **JLCPCB answered (2026-11-17):** v0.2 needs **Standard PCBA** on a 4 x 4
  panel. The headers are hand- or selective-soldered at $0.0164 per joint plus
  $3.58 per order. There is no fixed clearance between pins and SMT parts; it is
  checked in DFM. Details are in the README, under Pixel PCB.

## v0.2 changes

All of these come from the README. Sources: "Feedback for Pixel PCB v0.2" (House
holder section), the Pixel PCB section, and Interconnect.

1. **Headers:** vertical, on the bottom, in two stacked rows through the center.
   - J1: male XFCN PZ254V-11-03P, **C2937625**. Pin row east-west, centered on
     x = 0, at holder y = +2.1 mm.
   - J2: female 8.5 mm XFCN PM254V-11-03-H85, **C541850**. Pin row at holder
     y = −1.525 mm.
   - KiCad Y is flipped relative to the holder model. Confirm which row sits
     toward the cord notch (south) against `hardware/holder/params.scad` and
     `pixel_mock.scad` before placing.
   - Footprints: KiCad stock `PinHeader_1x03_P2.54mm_Vertical` and
     `PinSocket_1x03_P2.54mm_Vertical`, placed on B.Cu.
   - Keep power on the middle pin. Either pin order works.
2. **Top-side keep-outs:**
   - The six pin tails stick up about 1.4 - 1.6 mm through the middle, in a
     roughly 5.7 x 4.3 mm patch.
   - Keep about **1.5 - 2 mm** of clearance from those pins to SMT parts until
     JLCPCB gives a number.
   - Keep the edge ring clear for the holder's hook lips: top-side parts at
     r ≤ 9.25 mm.
3. **Orientation feature** (notch, flat or small hole) at the south edge, to match
   the holder. Check `hardware/holder/holder.scad` for what the holder expects.
4. **Center the LED cluster.** v0.1's sat about 2.5 mm south of center.
5. **White solder mask.** v0.1 was green.
6. **Silkscreen version: "v0.2".** The release goes in `production/v0.2/` with a
   MANIFEST, following v0.1's pattern.
7. **Keep the v0.1 circuit unless tomorrow's tests change it.** That covers U1
   (WS2814F), D1 (BAT54S), R1 2.7k, R2 4.7k (was 1k), R3 100, R4 120, C1 100n, C2 1u
   50 V, and D2-D4 HL-5050RGBW. Update J1/J2 LCSC numbers in the schematic.

## Assembly (decided: Standard PCBA, 4 x 4 panel)

- Build a panel of 4 x 4 boards with mouse bites, 5 mm rails and fiducials. It
  must be at least 70 x 70 mm and at most 250 x 250 mm. Two panels of 16 cover 22
  houses plus spares.
- Check placement feasibility first, because the pin keep-out may conflict with
  the edge keep-out:
  - The pin pads span about x ±3.4 mm and y −2.4 to +3.0 mm (1.7 mm pads).
  - A 5 x 5 mm LED placed east or west of them, even 1 mm away, reaches past
    r 9.25 mm at its corners.
  - North and south placements are tight too (about r 9.3 with 1 mm clearance).
  - The holder allows D3 to reach r 9.62 mm in the south mouth.
  - If the LEDs don't fit, raise it with the user before shrinking clearances.
- **Feasibility result (2026-11-17, computed, not yet placed in KiCad):** with the
  stack centered, an east/west LED gets at most 0.52 mm from the pads (1.05 mm
  from the pins), and the north LED 0.96 / 1.49 mm. Proposed fix, pending the
  holder team: move the whole header stack **x −1.0, y −0.45 mm** (holder frame).
  That gives N 1.41 / 1.94, S 1.46 / 1.99 and E 1.52 / 2.05 mm (pad / pin). Put
  the LEDs north, south and east, with U1, D1 and the passives on the west side.
  - **Holder team approved (2026-11-17):** dx from −1.0 to −1.3 mm (they prefer
    −1.15 to −1.30), dy −0.45. Holder: J1 (dx, +1.650), J2 (dx, −1.975). KiCad:
    J1 center pin (dx, −1.650), J2 center pin (dx, +1.975). On their side, the
    cheek trim `circle(r = 8.15)` at `holder.scad:124` widens to r 9.45. They
    report that coupon v0.3 results still hold and the cords route fine. Optional:
    with J2's DOUT on the east end (`pm_dx_sign = +1`), the plug sleeve sits
    0.275 mm east, which gives more west margin. They also suggest a Ø2.0 south
    notch at KiCad (0, +10.0), matching `coupon.scad`. That notch would cut into
    the south LED's space (its inner edge is at y 9.0), limiting it to about
    0.88 mm from the pads.
  - **Decided (user, 2026-11-17):** dx = **−1.20 mm**, dy = −0.45. KiCad, relative
    to the board center: J1 center pin at (−1.20, −1.650), J2 center pin at
    (−1.20, +1.975). This leaves the east LED 1.72 mm from the pads (2.25 from the
    pins), and U1 0.64 mm from the west pads. **No edge notch:** orientation is a
    south-pointing silkscreen arrow only, since the off-center plugs already key
    the board. Plan item 3 is replaced by this. Tell the holder team dx = −1.20
    and no notch.
  - Moving further west helps only the east LED. North and south are set by the
    y offset. The ceiling is about x −1.3: past that, U1 (3.8 mm courtyard, turned
    on end) can't keep 0.5 mm from the pads inside r 9.25.
  - Holder impact: add the offset to `j1_x/j2_x/j1_y/j2_y` in `params.scad`. The
    guide cheeks in `holder.scad` (lines 106-141) are hard-coded at x = 0 and need
    one translate. The cleats stay at x ±2.85.
  - Edge limits: the capture lip's inner face is at r 9.48, and the lip sits only
    0.75 mm above the board there. So the LEDs stay inside r 9.25, while the 0402
    parts may reach about r 9.85 under the lip.
- Low parts (0402 resistors and capacitors, SOT-23, FSOP-8) can sit nearer the
  pins than the 1.6 mm-tall LEDs, because JLCPCB's concern is iron access.
- The user is not set up for hand soldering. JLCPCB should assemble everything,
  including the through-hole headers.

## Layout status (2026-11-17)

First full layout done; KiCad DRC shows 0 errors and 0 unconnected. The only
warning is the old `JLCJLCJLCJLC` back-silk text.
- **J1/J2:** stock vertical footprints on B.Cu. J1 pins at y 98.35 and J2 pins at
  y 101.975, both at x 96.26 / 98.80 / 101.34 (+12 V in the middle). J1 has
  DATA_IN on the **west** end and GND east. J2 has DATA_OUT on the **east** end
  (the holder team's `pm_dx_sign = +1`) and GND west. J1's pin order was my
  choice, since the plan says either works. The user said (2026-11-17) both may
  flip to whatever helps DFM. Pin order doesn't move the pads, so it doesn't change
  any part-to-pad gap. The current order is kept because DATA_IN sits next to
  R2/D1, which keeps the routes short.
- **LEDs:** D2 north (100, 93.6, 180°), D3 east (106.4, 100, 90°), D4 south
  (100, 106.75, 0°). The series chain runs D2 → D3 → D4 → U1, so each LED-to-LED
  link is 4 parallel diagonals. Largest corner radius: D2 and D3 r 9.25, D4 r 9.59
  (in the south mouth).
- **West side:** U1 (92.87, 100, 90°), 0.64 mm from the J1/J2 pads. NW corner: D1,
  R2, R3, C1. SW corner: R1, C2, R4. Every part is inside r 9.25 except D4.
- **Routing:** Freerouting 2.4.1, headless (`-de pixel.dsn -do pixel.ses -mp 100`),
  13 vias. The DSN was exported from the KiCad GUI, because `pcbnew.LoadBoard()`
  segfaults in the 10.0.7 Python bindings, even on KiCad's own demo boards.
  The DSN/SES files are not committed.
- **Silkscreen:** "v0.2" at (105.4, 105.9), and a 1.4 mm south-pointing arrow
  between the header rows at x 100.07.
- **Plan item 4 (center the LEDs) is not met; the user accepted this on
  2026-11-17, since centering isn't a hard constraint.** It's a trade-off: the LED
  centroid is about 2.1 mm **east** of center (v0.1 was about 2.9 mm south). With
  the pins in the middle, the LEDs can only sit north, south and east, and moving
  D2 or D4 west pushes their corners past r 9.25.
- **Closest SMT parts to the header pads (top side, where the headers are
  soldered):** U1 0.64 mm (courtyard), R1 about 0.73, R3 about 0.81, D1 about
  0.92. JLCPCB's DFM decides whether these are acceptable.
- **Still to do:** white mask (set on the JLCPCB order), the 4 x 4 panel, the
  `production/v0.2/` package and MANIFEST, and README TBD updates.

## Panel (draft 2026-11-18)

- KiKit 1.8.1, preset `panel/panel.json`. The panel is 4 x 4, 100.1 x 115.1 mm,
  with a 5 mm frame, 3 mm horizontal crossbars between rows, and 2 mm milled gaps.
  It has 4 tooling holes (Ø1.152 mm, JLCPCB's size), 3 fiducials, and a 1 mm mill
  radius.
- Tabs: one 3 mm mouse-bite tab at **north** and one at **south** per board.
  These are the only edge spots outside the holder's jaws (north gap 76 - 104°,
  south mouth). Holes are Ø0.5 at 0.8 mm pitch, centered on the board edge
  (offset 0), because the default inward offset put the holes inside D4's
  courtyard. The holes sit 0.5 mm from D4's body.
- Frame and crossbar break lines are off, to keep the panel stiff for assembly.
- Decided (user, 2026-11-18): cut the tabs with flush cutters, don't snap them,
  because of D4. Order-number text dropped entirely, since on the v0.1 order
  JLCPCB didn't print one and "Remove Mark" was used. The per-board
  `JLCJLCJLCJLC` back-silk text is to be deleted too.
- KiCad DRC on the panel: 0 errors.

## Tooling notes (from v0.1)

- The project targets **KiCad 10**. The v0.1 files are saved in KiCad 10's format.
- **Routing:** export DSN with `pcbnew.ExportSpecctraDSN`, route with
  Freerouting's bundled launcher, then import the session in KiCad (File →
  Import → Specctra Session). Konnect's own DSN export refused the round board.
- **Rules:** `pixel.kicad_dru` and the board setup enforce JLCPCB's limits. Power
  netclass 0.4 mm. Signals 0.2 mm, with VDD allowed down to 0.15 mm near U1.
- **JLCPCB export:** Konnect's `export_manufacturing_package` with `fab_house`
  jlcpcb, BOM fields `Value,Reference,Footprint,LCSC`, and
  `jlcpcb_cpl_corrections_path` = `jlcpcb_cpl_corrections.json` (U1 +270,
  D1 +180). Check JLCPCB's placement preview.
- **LCSC lookups:** the kicad-happy `lcsc` skill's `search_lcsc.py` and
  `fetch_datasheet_lcsc.py`. The jlcsearch `/headers/list.json?pitch=2.54&num_pins=3`
  endpoint lists header candidates. `https://wmsc.lcsc.com/ftps/wm/product/detail?productCode=Cxxxx`
  gives live LCSC stock.
