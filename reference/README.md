# Reference

Source measurements and photos behind the "Physical constraints" section of the
top-level README. Unlike `archive/`, this is current data. When something here
changes the constraints, update the README too.

- `houses/2026-11-08_house_measurements_notes.pdf`: scanned handwritten notes,
  7 houses. Hole diameter, wall thickness and recess ("inlet") depth.
- `houses/2021_house_measurements.xlsx`: wall thickness, 12 houses.
- `servo_plugs/`: photos of the purchased servo extension cable's plugs and
  the header samples (C492411 male, C2897385 female, both right-angle), taken
  2026-11-14. The photos were resized and their metadata stripped.
  - `female_on_ra_header.jpg`: female plug mated on the male header.
  - `male_mouth.jpg`: the male plug's mouth, pins inside the sleeve.
  - `male_mouth_sketch.jpg`: sketch of the mouth: 10.25 x 4 mm, with a
    1.4 x 2.2 mm key block in one corner at the signal-wire end.
  - `male_and_ra_socket.jpg`: male plug and female header side by side.
  - `male_on_ra_socket_top.jpg`, `male_on_ra_socket_edge.jpg`,
    `male_on_ra_socket_end.jpg`: male plug mated on the female header, from
    three sides.
